Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Little Perch works fully without signing in. Signing in with Google is optional and only used to sync your saved list across devices.

A listing is out of date or wrong

Every listing links to the organization's own website so you can confirm current details before you go or register. If something in the app looks wrong, email us the listing name and what's off, and we'll fix it. You can also use the "Report an issue" option inside the app.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only for the "Near Me" screen, which shows nearby playgrounds, pools, libraries, museums, and events on a map. Your location is used on your device only and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can use the rest of the app without granting location access.

How current is the data?

Listings are reviewed regularly against each organization's own site. The app shows the last-refreshed date at the bottom of the Calendar and Home screens. Schedules — especially for seasonal programs, camps, and pool hours — can change, so always confirm with the organization before relying on a specific time.

Deleting your data

If you never signed in, all your data (saved activities, filters, preferences) lives only on your device — deleting the app removes it. If you signed in with Google and want your account data deleted from our backend, email us from the address you signed in with and we'll remove it.
