LittlePerch Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Short version: LittlePerch has no backend server and does not collect, store, or sell your personal data on any server we operate. Almost everything you do in the app (saved activities, your preferences, filters) stays on your own device. The only data that leaves your device goes directly to Google, for the specific features described below — never to us.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes the data practices of the LittlePerch mobile app ("LittlePerch," "the app," "we," "us"), a directory of San Francisco kids' and family activities. LittlePerch is designed to be used by parents and caregivers to find and plan activities for their children — it is not designed for direct, unsupervised use by children.

What we collect and store

On your device only

When you use LittlePerch, the following is saved locally on your device (not on any server we control):

  • Onboarding preferences you set — age ranges and interests, used to personalize recommendations
  • Activities you save/favorite
  • Neighborhood/area filters you apply
  • Whether you're signed in with Google, and basic profile info from that sign-in (see below)

This data is stored using your device's local storage. It is never transmitted to LittlePerch or any server we operate. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Google Sign-In (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, LittlePerch requests basic profile information directly from Google — your name, email address, and profile photo — using Google's own Sign-In service. This is used only to personalize your experience within the app on your device (for example, so your saved activities feel tied to "you"). We do not receive your Google password, and we do not store this profile information on any server — it stays on your device, tied to your local app data. You can sign out at any time, which clears this locally.

Third-party services we use

Certain features in LittlePerch work by making requests directly from your device to these third-party services. We don't see or store the content of these requests ourselves — they go straight from your device to the service listed:

  • Google Maps & Places — powers the in-app map, directions links (which open Google Maps), and playground photos/ratings. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In — see above. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — powers the weather widget. We send a fixed San Francisco coordinate, not your actual location — this service does not receive any personal or device-identifying information from the app.
  • Individual activity/event organizers' own websites — when you view an activity's detail page, the app may briefly fetch that organization's own public web page to display their event photo. No personal information about you is sent in this request.

What we don't do

  • We don't run advertising or ad networks in this app.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We don't have a server that stores your personal data, account information, or usage history.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Location

LittlePerch does not currently request or use your device's precise location. The weather shown in the app is for San Francisco generally, not your specific location.

Children's privacy

LittlePerch is intended for use by parents and caregivers, not for direct, unsupervised use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the information below and we will address it.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data (saved activities, preferences, sign-in state).
  • Sign out: use the Sign Out option in the app to clear your local Google sign-in state at any time.
  • Location: the app does not request location access, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
